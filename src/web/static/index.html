Auscultation Acoustic Processing

Dataset Architecture & System Integrity

LubDub Murmur maintains strict physical and algorithmic separation between datasets. The CirCor DigiScope dataset powers heart murmur classification, clinical outcome prediction, and acoustic fingerprint similarity search. The HFrEF EKO DUO dataset (Zenodo 16934966) powers multimodal electrocardiogram and 4-valve phonocardiogram systolic function screening. Patient identifiers are never conflated or merged.

About LubDub Murmur

Heart Sound Analysis System & PhysioNet CirCor DigiScope Architecture

🫀

Multi-Location Auscultation

Processes standardized multi-channel acoustic phonocardiogram signals across five anatomical valve sites (Aortic, Mitral, Pulmonary, Tricuspid, and Other).

🧠

Masked Attention Fusion

Features a shared 2D CNN encoder and masked scaled dot-product attention fusion, dynamically handling missing cardiac locations with zero patient data leakage.

🎯

Multi-Task Diagnostics

Dual prediction heads simultaneously evaluate Heart Murmur presence and overall Clinical Outcome based on acoustic spectral-temporal representations.

🧬

Fingerprint V2 & Similarity

Derives deterministic 1,264-dimensional acoustic fingerprints (MFCC + Mel + AST embeddings) to compute exact cosine similarity against reference cohorts.

⚠️ Medical Disclaimer: LubDub Murmur is developed for research, education, and algorithmic benchmarking on the PhysioNet CirCor DigiScope PCG dataset. Model predictions represent probabilistic classifications of phonocardiographic acoustic signals and do not constitute a definitive clinical diagnosis.
